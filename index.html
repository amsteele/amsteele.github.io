About

I study circumstellar material using observational data and computational science. My research focuses on polluted white dwarfs -- stars that bear chemical signatures of planetary material -- using high-resolution spectroscopy, radiative transfer modeling, and photometric time series analyses.

Alongside research, I build tools (Python pipelines, scientific databases, and public-facing interfaces) to make data more usable and accessible. I also create graphics to communicate scientific concepts in an alternative medium.

Focus Polluted white dwarfs, planetary remnants, spectroscopy
Tools Python, spectral modeling, large survey data
